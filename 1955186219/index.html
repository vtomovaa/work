Let’s break down what prop trading actually is — quick and simple.

Prop trading: modern prop firms allow you to trade with simulated funds instead of risking your own capital. This aims to help people learn, grow and get access to opportunities they might have normally been unable to take advantage of.

How it works?

Your journey starts with a challenge – prove your skills and risk management through evaluation stages designed to test your trading knowledge, risk management, and decision-making skills. Once you succeed, you'll gain access to simulated funded account, support, and resources that can result in payouts.

Add-ons

Each add-on provides traders with specific advantages for a customized trading experience:

Double/Higher Leverage

Increases the leverage available for some or all asset classes in both the challenge and funded account.

Trade on the Weekends

Enables traders to hold positions and trade over the weekend, available if weekend trading is initially disallowed.

No Stop Loss Requirement

Removes the mandatory stop loss requirement for every position, offered if the default setting requires a stop loss.

Higher Profit Share

Offers an increased percentage of the profit share to the trader upon reaching the funded stage, enhancing earnings potential.

News Trading

Allows traders to hold positions and/or actively trade during major news events, applicable if such trading is restricted by default.

Trader Payout 14 Days Guarantee

Is an add-on that enables traders to request their payouts in 14 days, shortening the standard payout timeframe.
